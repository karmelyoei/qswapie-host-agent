# QSwapie Host Agent: 2 minute pitch outline

**OUTLINE ONLY, rewrite in your own words**

Event: Agentic AI Hack by Jamie, Berlin, Open Agentic Track. Demo length: 2 min.

## 0:00 to 0:20 Hook and problem
- Walk-in waiting is dead time. Guests stand around or give up.
- Owners lose guests who walk away.
- Chairs sit idle when the next guest is not there yet.
- Quick intro: Karmel, founder of QSwapie (a live digital queue, scan a QR code, see your place).
- Say it in your own words: [one sentence about a time you waited too long]

## 0:20 to 1:20 Live demo beats
- Show the 3 panels: queue (left), guest phone (middle), owner view and activity feed (right).
- Click "Simulate rush". Maybe speed up the clock.
- Watch the agent act on its own. Nobody asked it.
- Point at the activity feed: every action has a one line reason.
- Honest wait range on each guest (for example 12 to 20 min), not a fake single number.
- Proactive message: "come back in about 10 min", then "you're next, head back now".
- A no-show gets flagged, the line moves up.
- Tap "in a hurry": agent finds a guest who will swap later and proposes it to both. No money in the demo.
- Owner alert: "queue is 40+ min deep, pause new joins?" and "2 no-shows, call the next 2".
- Show the numbers: idle chair minutes, no-shows handled, swaps done.
- Say it in your own words: [what you want the judges to notice here]

## 1:20 to 1:40 Why proactive matters
- Reactive queue apps wait for the guest to check the screen.
- The agent acts first: it watches the queue and decides when to speak.
- Result: fewer idle chairs, fewer lost guests.
- Backup: offline rule based fallback keeps the demo running if the API fails.
- Say it in your own words: [why you trust an agent here]

## 1:40 to 1:50 Honesty line
- QSwapie existed before this hack.
- The agent, the UI and the simulation were built today.
- All data is simulated (fake first names).
- Uses Claude with tool use.

## 1:50 to 2:00 What is next
- Connect to the real QSwapie queue.
- SMS and WhatsApp messages to real guests.
- Voice, maybe with partner tech (Reson8), to be decided.
- Pilot venues in Berlin: barbershops and cafes.
- Say it in your own words: [closing line and ask]

- Pick a speed setting that fits in about 60 seconds.
